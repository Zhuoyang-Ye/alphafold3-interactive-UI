Automated Database Downloader

AlphaFold 3 searches large biological databases (UniRef90, BFD, PDB, RNAcentral) for homologous evolutionary sequences. Choose between instant mode or the complete research databases.

⚡ Mode 1: Instant Sequence-Only Mode (0 GB download)

Predict single sequences and basic structures right away with zero database downloads! In the app, choose New experiment → Sequence-only prediction.

🔬 Mode 2: Full Research Databases (~250 GB download / ~630 GB unpacked)

Automate downloading and unpacking all sequence databases to your local drive or external SSD:

On macOS / Linux (Terminal):

bash fetch_databases_macos.sh ~/public_databases

On Windows (PowerShell):

powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File .\apps\windows\fetch_databases_windows.ps1

Obtaining Model Parameters

AlphaFold 3's neural network weights (~1 GB) are provided directly by Google DeepMind under their terms of use.

1. Request Access

Complete the parameter request form on Google DeepMind's GitHub repository: AlphaFold 3 Model Parameters Request ↗

2. Save to Your Models Folder

Place the downloaded file af3.bin.zst in your user models directory:

mkdir -p ~/models && mv ~/Downloads/af3.bin.zst ~/models/

All predictions run 100% locally on your own machine. Your sequences never leave your computer.
BEGINNER GUIDE

Exploring Molecules in 3D

🧬 What is AlphaFold 3?

AlphaFold 3 is Google DeepMind's AI system that predicts how proteins, DNA, RNA, ligands, and medicines assemble and fold in 3D space with atomic precision.

🌀 What do the 3D shapes mean?

• Spirals (Alpha Helices): Structural springs that support protein architectures.
• Flat Arrows (Beta Sheets): Broad, rigid sheets that form molecular platforms.
• Thin Loops: Flexible connecting bridges.
• Small Sticks: Chemical ligands, vitamins, and medicines docking into active sites.

🎨 What do the colors mean?

• By Chain: Each separate molecule (e.g. Protein, DNA, Drug) is colored differently.
• By Confidence: Blue = AI is very confident in this region; Orange = AI is guessing (often an unstructured, flexible region).

🖱️ Navigation Controls

• Rotate: Left-click and drag across the viewer.
• Zoom: Scroll with mouse wheel or pinch trackpad.
• Pan: Shift-click and drag (or right-click and drag).
• Inspect: Click any atom to see its name and position!
